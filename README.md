# SiteShield | AI Construction Safety, PPE & Child Labor Protection Platform

An AI-powered construction safety monitoring, workforce legal compliance, and child labor protection platform. Features the official **SiteShield** branding with `contruction safty app logo.png`, mandatory age verification enforcing ILO Conventions 138/182, real-time computer vision detection simulation, step-by-step PPE verification, OSHA compliance analysis, gamified safety badges, multilingual support (including full RTL Arabic and Urdu), comprehensive light and dark theme background/text inversion, and automated recurring safety timers.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🪪 Initial Login Gate & Age Section (Child Labor Protection)
- **Mandatory Age Verification**: Every worker must provide and verify their age.
- **Strict Child Labor Law Enforcement**:
  - In accordance with **ILO Convention No. 138 & 182** and **OSHA regulations**, hazardous construction work is strictly prohibited for individuals under 18 years of age.
  - If an age under 18 is entered, an immediate siren sounds and the **Child Labor Violation Blocker** prevents entry.
  - Includes a 1-click **"Test Minor (Age 15)"** button to demonstrate the legal block in action.
- **Name & Age Reflection**: Once verified, the worker's name and age are dynamically reflected throughout the entire platform:
  - Header profile badge
  - Step 1 of the PPE Verification Gate
  - Shift Entry Pass (Step 4)
  - Leaderboard row highlighted with `(You)`
  - Safe work permit reports
- **Switch Worker / Sign Out**: Quick-access button in the header to switch profiles or log out anytime.

### 2. 🏗️ Construction Site Hazard & PPE Scanner (Photos & Videos)
- **Image & Video Uploads**: Drag and drop any construction site photo (JPG, PNG, WebP) or video (MP4, WebM).
- **1-Click Pre-Loaded Test Scenarios**:
  - *Scenario 1: Scaffolding Hazard* (Missing guardrails, missing helmets, trip hazards).
  - *Scenario 2: Compliant Crew* (Full PPE, steel-toe boots, clear pathways - 96/100 score).
  - *Scenario 3: High-Rise Fall Risk* (Unprotected 120ft drop, missing personal fall arrest system).
  - *Scenario 4: Excavation Trench Collapse* (Trench >5ft missing shoring, spoil pile violation - OSHA 1926.652).
- **Computer Vision Overlays**: Bounding boxes with confidence ratings, severity tags, and OSHA regulation citations.
- **Immediate Hazard Siren**: Real-time visual strobe banner and audio alert when critical violations are detected.
- **Export Official Safety Report (PDF)**: 1-click print/export formatted for compliance officers.

### 3. 📸 Step-by-Step PPE Gear Verification Gate
- **4-Stage Verification Procedure**:
  1. *Worker ID, Verified Age & Shift Confirmation* (Auto-populated with logged-in user).
  2. *Live Camera / Selfie Capture* (Supports real webcam via WebRTC or 1-click instant test snapshots).
  3. *Sequential Head-to-Toe AI Scan* with animated scanning radar:
     - 🪖 Hard Hat with locked chin strap (ANSI Z89.1 Type 1 Class E).
     - 🦺 High-Visibility Reflective Vest (ANSI/ISEA 107 Class 2/3).
     - 🥽 Safety Eyewear & Face Protection (ANSI Z87.1).
     - 🧤 Cut-resistant Gloves & Steel-Toe Boots.
  4. *Clearance Gate*:
     - **Access Granted**: Confetti explosion, triumph audio, digital shift pass with barcode, **+50 safety points** awarded!
     - **Access Denied**: Audible alarm siren, flashing warning, and explicit rectification checklist.
